Data & modeling notes

Before unlocking, the viewer shows precomputed nominal CFD. Authorized users download the model and predict steady temperature and air velocity on their GPU. Five presets fill in the eight controls.

Particles follow predicted streamlines, with color indicating air temperature. Particle count does not represent airflow rate. Slow blades affect only the fan animation, not particle speed.

Racks and the heat exchanger use equivalent thermal models; displayed temperatures are not CPU junction temperatures. The office, landscaping and decorative equipment geometry are visual context and do not participate in the thermal-flow calculation.

Air, racks and cooling plates each use their own temperature range from the current prediction. All six racks share one rack scale. Compare the numerical legends when comparing results.

Supply and return air temperatures and return airflow are calculated from the predicted field. Heat removal and return-water temperature are estimated from predicted cooling-plate temperatures, water flow and the equivalent heat-exchange model. Inlet water temperature is fixed at 15°C.

Prediction errors may increase as more parameters fall outside the training range and deviate further from it. The model has not yet been calibrated against actual equipment.
